# Dataset inventory (invented example)

- Lakeside: columns trip_id, start_time, end_time, start_station, end_station; Mar-Feb, twelve months; a trip is a rental of at least 60 seconds.
- Northgate: columns id, started, ended, from_station, to_station, user_type; same twelve months; a trip is any rental, including test unlocks.
- Harbor: columns ride_id, start, stop, origin, destination; same twelve months; a trip is a rental of at least 60 seconds.
